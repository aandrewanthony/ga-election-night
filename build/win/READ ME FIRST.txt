GA ELECTION NIGHT FOR WINDOWS
=============================

1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All..." (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "GA Election Night".
3. Click "Install". It installs itself for your account with Start menu and desktop
   shortcuts, then opens. You can delete the zip and extracted folder afterwards.

First run: paste your IPTV .m3u link (the same one Dial TV uses) or open the .m3u file.
The app finds GPB, WSB (ABC 2), WXIA (NBC 11) and Atlanta CBS on its own; click a
station's name on any tile to change it.

Uninstalling: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GA Election Night > Uninstall.
If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
